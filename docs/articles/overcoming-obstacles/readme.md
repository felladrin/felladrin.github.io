# Overcoming obstacles

_Author unknown. This is a story my parents showed me when I was young. I tried to trace its origin without success; it seems to have circulated anonymously in Brazil, in the 1990s or 2000s. If you happen to know its source, please let me know, so I can credit it properly._

Two men were talking. One was very sad for not being able to reach his goals, and got irritated with the other, who insisted on telling him that nothing was impossible to achieve. At some point, the sad man decided to end that discussion by giving completely irrefutable arguments.

He said:

"All right, nothing is impossible? Then tell me, is there any possibility of, for example, a man taking flight?"

"Of course there is. What do airplanes exist for?", the friend answered calmly.

The other laughed sarcastically and said:

"Fine, but then you need the help of engines. What about without the help of engines?"

The answer came immediately:

"Hang gliding."

A bit nervous, the sad man insisted:

"But then you need wings and a high place. And if I can't have any of that?"

The answer came even faster than before:

"Hot air balloon."

Already red with anger, the man hit the table and said:

"But suppose I don't have a balloon, or a hang glider, or an airplane, or anything. Flying by myself, is that possible?"

The other man thought for a couple of seconds and said:

"Well, in a low-gravity zone, you can practically fly. Or in a wind tunnel, or with a magnetic suit against a magnetic platform of the same polarity."

The sad man got furious, stood up, hit the table and said:

"But what if I have none, none of that?"

The other thought, thought, and thought for a long time, and answered, with his hand on his friend's shoulder:

"From what I can see, your goal is not to fly. It's to come up with every possible obstacle and excuse for not taking flight."
