# Death

_Rescued from my 2008 blog, "Armazém de Idéias", and republished here so it doesn't get lost. Translated from Portuguese; otherwise, it's as I wrote it back then._

What is this thing that makes even the bravest of men tremble? Death is, and has always been, one of the greatest fears of living beings. That's quite sensible, since the unknown frightens our mind and bombards it with countless ideas about what we may find ahead. It has been like this forever. It's curiosity that pushes us to explore the unknown. But who would like to experience death? It's not like a game; we don't have a "restart" button. It's a one-way trip, even if life, in some way, goes on. Culture and religion guide the many different thoughts about what death must be. Some mourn, some celebrate, but the fact is that those who are gone will never again be among us.

If it's inevitable, why fear it? Or better: why wait for it? We should make the most of the time we were given here on Earth, following what our senses, principles, or doctrines command. Because one day, in the end, everything ends.
