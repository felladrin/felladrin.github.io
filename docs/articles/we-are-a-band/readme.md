# We Are a Band!

_Rescued from my 2009 blog and republished here so it doesn't get lost. Translated from Portuguese; otherwise, it's as I wrote it back then._

Comparisons between the Summa Imperii project and the game Rock Legend.

## Promotion

In the game, the band starts by handing out flyers in front of clubs and spreading posters on the streets. After a few shows, it gets famous, and the promotion becomes "self-sufficient", since they receive proposals for interviews on TV, radio, and newspapers. At this point, word of mouth from the fans does all the rest.

In the beginning, we have to work hard on promotion. It's tiring, but the better we perform at this point, the faster we become known.

## Motivation

Motivation is what gives us the strength to move forward with the project. There will always be demotivating factors, proportional to the project's age. We have to keep this in mind, think positive, and hold on to the motivating parts. The demotivation of a single member can harm the whole group. Unity influences this part a lot.

## Members

Each person has their virtues, flaws, preferences, talents, and life experience. We have to consider a person a lot before making them a member, because they can make all the difference in the project.

## Disagreements between members

It's impossible to please everyone, since each one has their own ideas and principles. The more people in the project, the higher the chances of disagreements. What we can do to soften this is keep in mind that ideas must be chosen based on the game's proposal, not on the taste of one or a few members. So only those who agree with the proposals, and with what has already been decided, should join the project.

## How to find inspiration

Watching movies, listening to their soundtracks, playing other games of the same style, reading RPG books, reading forums about these subjects, and so on.

Inspiration has its highs and lows, and when it's high, we should seize the opportunity to write new ideas or improve the existing ones.

## Always innovate - Composing new songs

Just like people can get tired of a band that always plays the same songs, players get tired of games that always offer the same things. At a minimum, we should have some tricks up our sleeves, to use when "the audience is leaving". The ideal is to have several "tricks" so that, by rotating them, things take longer to repeat.

## Harmony - Training level

The members must know everything they can about the project ("be ready for the show"). It's not possible to memorize everything, and we forget things over time. But it's good, from time to time, to review everything that has already been decided. It's like a band: if the songs are not rehearsed, the show will be a disaster.

The knowledge among members must be balanced. If the difference in project knowledge between one member and another is too big, the group will "walk crooked".

## Special effects during the show

Presentation means a lot. I think this part can be compared to the game's presentation on the website. The website is as important as the game, and we must always emphasize this to the players. It should be cozy, so people enjoy staying on it and are not ashamed of leaving it open.

## Experience

Our first shows will never be as good as our last ones. Over time, we improve our performance. In the case of events, the more events we run, the better the next ones will be. It would even be nice to create some simulations of basic events to serve as practical training for the game masters. It could be a theory test and a practical one, involving our rules and systems.

## Days off - Fatigue

Routine is tiring! Everyone has to take some time to relax the mind. This must be done so the project doesn't become a burden. We could pick two days of the week when it would be fine not to show up at the forum, that is, on those days no topic needing urgent attention would be posted.
